## Deletions

The pre-rendered figures under `demo/BGMM_data/` — `compare_i=0_j=1.pdf` … `compare_i=3_j=4.pdf`
(10 files), `bgm_probability_of_each_component.pdf` and `ergodic.png`. All are *outputs*: the two
scripts write them and nothing in the corpus reads them. Running `build_density.py` then `sample.py`
recreates the whole set.

`density.pcl` and `ergodic_data.txt` were kept: `ergodic_data.txt` is the input to both scripts, and
`density.pcl` is the model `sample.py` loads.

Advanced Data Analytics · The semester project

The semester project

One project, carried out individually — the whole assessment for this course.

What it is

The work
One project, carried out individually You choose a question, find data, apply methods from the course, and write it up. The project is the whole assessment for this course.
What you hand in
A paper, the data, the code, and a 15-minute recording The paper is about ten pages in the SIAM conference style, with eight required sections. The recording presents the project in your own voice.
Two dates
A 200-word proposal, then the project The proposal is signed off by the teaching team before you start work. Dates are confirmed at the start of the semester and announced here and on Moodle at the same time.
AI tools
Allowed, and expected — but declared Use assistants for code and prose. List every tool you used, and what you used it for, in the appendix. Undeclared use is misconduct; declared use is normal practice.
If the result is negative
An honest null result can score full marks A hypothesis that data does not support is a real finding, and a well-told account of why it failed is worth more than a result that was helped along.

The two documents

Requirements
Requirements for the semester project (PDF) The binding text: deadlines, what is submitted, how it is assessed. Where this page and the requirements differ, the requirements are what counts.
Shipping guidelines
Shipping your project (PDF) Practical advice, and not binding: folder layout, the README, reproducibility, the recording, and the one test to run before you submit.

Gallery

Selected projects are published here, with their authors' permission and credited to them. Consent is asked for at submission, is entirely optional, and has no bearing on the grade.

The gallery opens with the first consenting projects from this cohort. Until then, the requirements above describe the standard the work is held to.
